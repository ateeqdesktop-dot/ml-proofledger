# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want ML ProofLedger to be a welcoming, technically rigorous project. Participants should communicate with respect, assume good faith, focus on evidence and ideas, and welcome constructive disagreement.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, threats, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Private information must not be published without explicit permission.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainer through the repository's private reporting channel. Reports will be reviewed fairly and handled with confidentiality where possible. Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
